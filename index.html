About us

Who We Are

World Merit Oujda Council is a local youth community connected to the international World Merit network. We bring together young people who are passionate about creating positive change in their communities and contributing to a better future.

Based in Oujda, in Morocco’s Oriental region, we aim to create opportunities for young people to learn, collaborate, take initiative and become active contributors to sustainable development.

We believe that young people are not only the leaders of tomorrow but also important actors in shaping change today.

Founded in 2026, the council is led by Maryem and Inass. Our members come from the Faculté des sciences juridiques, économiques et sociales in Oujda and beyond.

Our Mission

To empower young people to become active changemakers by fostering leadership, developing skills, encouraging civic engagement and supporting initiatives that address local and global challenges.

Our Vision

A generation of empowered, responsible and engaged young people who transform ideas into meaningful action and contribute to inclusive and sustainable communities.

Our Values

  • Empowerment: Enabling young people to recognize their potential and take initiative.
  • Collaboration: Building meaningful connections and working together towards shared goals.
  • Innovation: Encouraging creativity and new approaches to addressing challenges.
  • Inclusion: Promoting equal opportunities and valuing diversity.
  • Responsibility: Taking action with integrity and commitment.
  • Sustainability: Contributing to lasting positive change.

Our impact

Small Actions. Meaningful Change.

We believe that the value of an initiative lies not only in what it achieves today but also in the positive change it creates over time.

We aim to measure our contribution through:

  • Young people engaged.
  • Volunteers mobilized.
  • Initiatives implemented.
  • Partnerships developed.
  • Communities reached.
  • Sustainable Development Goals addressed.

Our impact indicators and achievements will be updated as activities are implemented and results are verified.
