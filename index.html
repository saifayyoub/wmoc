Where we come from

The World Merit vision

World Merit is a global network of young people who want to make a difference. Our Oujda Council is one of its local chapters.

Vision

A world beyond beliefs and borders, where millions of young adults become drivers of local change.

Our values

Inclusivity, member-driven and collaborative: every changemaker belongs, young people lead, and impact grows through partnership.

Our promise

Learn, act, connect, grow. We explore new skills, turn learning into projects, link up across borders and grow as individuals and teams.

The club

World Merit Oujda Council

We are the Oujda chapter of World Merit: a group of young people in Oujda who meet, learn and act together. We bring the global vision of World Merit into our own city through projects, workshops and a community that backs every member's ideas.

Founded in 2026, our council is led by Maryem and Inass. Our members come from the Faculté des sciences juridiques, économiques et sociales in Oujda and beyond.

  • Inclusivity. Every changemaker belongs, whatever their background.
  • Member-driven. Young people shape the direction of the council.
  • Collaboration. We build partnerships so impact multiplies.
  • Responsibility & action. We take on real local needs and turn ideas into projects, guided by the SDGs.
  • Learn · Act · Connect · Grow. The promise every World Merit council and member follows.
